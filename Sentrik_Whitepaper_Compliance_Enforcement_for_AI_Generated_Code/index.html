    The Compliance Gap in AI-Generated Code¶

    How Sentrik enforces regulatory requirements at the speed of AI development¶

    CATEGORY Compliance Enforcement & Audit Evidence Automation
    AUDIENCE Engineering, Security, and Compliance leaders in regulated industries
    PUBLISHED May 2026 — v1.0

    Executive Summary¶

    AI coding assistants have fundamentally changed the rate at which code is produced. Teams using GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, and Cursor report 40–80% increases in code output. Compliance programs have not kept pace.

    The problem is structural. AI models are trained to produce functional code. They are not trained against IEC 62304, HIPAA, EU AI Act, or MISRA C. They hallucinate security patterns, omit required documentation obligations, and introduce vulnerabilities at a rate Stanford researchers estimate at 36% of AI-generated output. When a developer produces ten times the code in the same day, the compliance team inherits ten times the review burden with no additional headcount.

    Sentrik is a governance runtime that sits in the same CI/CD pipeline as your existing tooling and enforces compliance automatically — scanning every commit against 24 regulatory and security frameworks, blocking non-compliant code before it merges, and generating audit evidence that would otherwise take weeks to assemble.

    This paper describes the problem in detail, how Sentrik solves it, and what teams in regulated industries have reported when they close the gap.

    The Problem: AI Makes Compliance Harder, Not Easier¶

    Volume overwhelms manual review¶

    Manual code review was already the bottleneck in compliant SDLC workflows before AI coding tools arrived. IEC 62304 software safety classes, HIPAA access control requirements, EU AI Act transparency obligations — these require specific patterns, specific documentation, and specific evidence. Reviewing for them takes time and expertise that most engineering teams do not have in abundance.

    AI-assisted development multiplies the volume of code requiring review without multiplying the reviewers. A team of ten engineers that previously produced 500 lines of production code per day may now produce 2,000 or more. The compliance function receives four times the surface area to audit at the same staffing level.

    AI models do not know your regulatory framework¶

    GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, and similar tools generate code that compiles and often passes unit tests. They do not know that your product is classified as a Class B medical device under IEC 62304 and requires specific error handling patterns, or that your HIPAA environment prohibits certain logging calls, or that your EU AI Act obligations require documentation of training data governance in the codebase.

    The models are not wrong to omit this — it is outside their training distribution. But the omission creates a systematic gap: every AI-generated file is a file that has not been checked against your regulatory requirements.

    Audit evidence is assembled by hand¶

    When a regulatory audit arrives, engineering teams typically spend four to six weeks reconstructing compliance evidence: which controls were in place, which findings were addressed, which code satisfies which requirement. This evidence exists in scattered form across git history, issue trackers, and CI logs. Assembling it is manual, error-prone, and expensive.

    How Sentrik Works¶

    Sentrik operates as a three-command governance runtime integrated into your existing developer workflow.

    Scan¶

    sentrik scan

    Sentrik scans your codebase against all enabled standards packs and produces a structured findings report. Every finding maps to a specific regulatory clause — not just a rule ID, but the actual section of IEC 62304, HIPAA, or OWASP that is violated. Findings are classified by severity (critical, high, medium, low, info) and include the exact file, line number, code snippet, and remediation guidance.

    Sentrik supports regex pattern matching, AST-based structural analysis via tree-sitter (Python, C/C++, Go, Rust, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Kotlin, and more), and LLM-powered semantic analysis for obligations that cannot be expressed as patterns. Parallel scanning with configurable worker threads keeps scan times fast on large codebases.

    Gate¶

    sentrik gate --git-range origin/main...HEAD --decorate-pr

    Sentrik gates pull requests in CI. When the gate runs against a PR diff, it evaluates only changed files, posts inline comments identifying the specific rule and regulatory clause, and exits non-zero if findings at or above the configured severity threshold are present. The PR cannot merge until findings are resolved or explicitly suppressed with documented justification.

    This makes compliance enforcement a property of the development workflow rather than a post-hoc audit step. Engineers fix findings before code ships, at the point when the cost of fixing is lowest.

    Gate configuration is declarative:

    # .sentrik/config.yaml
gate_fail_on:
  - critical
  - high
standards_packs:
  - owasp-top-10
  - hipaa
  - fda-iec-62304

    Attest¶

    sentrik attest

    Sentrik generates a cryptographically signed compliance attestation for every scan. The attestation records the scan timestamp, the rules evaluated, the findings present, the gate result, and a HMAC-SHA256 signature that makes the record tamper-evident. Attestations accumulate over time and become the audit trail that regulators and auditors review.

    For formal audits, Sentrik generates per-framework compliance reports, evidence map exports, and audit-ready ZIP bundles — all from the accumulated scan and attestation history.

    The Evidence Map: Proving What You Have, Not Just What You're Missing¶

    Most compliance tools produce a list of violations. Sentrik also produces an Evidence Map: a structured record of where your code satisfies each regulatory requirement.

    The Evidence Map maps specific files and line numbers to the regulatory clauses they satisfy. An auditor reviewing IEC 62304 compliance sees not just "no critical findings" but a positive record: which files implement required error handling, which modules satisfy software safety class requirements, which tests cover the required unit test obligations. This is the difference between a clean scan result and auditable evidence of compliance.

    The Evidence Map is available as an HTML report, a JSON export for GRC platform integration, and a dashboard view with per-framework filtering.

    24 Frameworks. 595 Rules. Out of the Box.¶

    Sentrik ships with rules covering every major software compliance framework relevant to regulated industries:

    Healthcare and Life Sciences (Team tier) - IEC 62304 — 31 rules for medical device software lifecycle - HIPAA — 25 rules for ePHI protection under 45 CFR Part 164 - IEC 81001-5-1 — 20 rules for health software cybersecurity (Organization tier) - ISO 14971 — 18 rules for medical device risk management (Organization tier) - 21 CFR Part 11 — 16 rules for FDA electronic records (Organization tier)

    Financial Services (Team tier) - PCI DSS v4.0 — 33 rules for cardholder data protection - ISO 27001:2022 — 32 rules for information security management - SOC 2 — 30 rules (Free tier)

    AI Governance (Team tier) - EU AI Act — 23 rules for high-risk AI systems: risk management, transparency, human oversight, data governance - NIST AI RMF — 16 rules for AI risk management (NIST AI 600-1)

    Cloud Infrastructure (Team tier) - Cloud IaC — 30 Terraform security rules for AWS, GCP, and Azure: credentials, public storage, IAM, encryption

    Public Sector and Defense (Team/Org tier) - NIST 800-53 Rev 5 — 21 rules for federal security controls - CMMC 2.0 — 19 rules for defense contractor cybersecurity

    Automotive, Aviation, Embedded (Organization tier) - ISO 26262 — 23 rules for automotive functional safety - DO-178C — 22 rules for airborne software - MISRA C/C++ — 21 rules for safety-critical C and C++ coding standards - GDPR — 25 rules for data protection and privacy (Team tier)

    Rules are expressed in a declarative YAML schema and can be extended with custom packs for organization-specific requirements.

    Integrations¶

    CI/CD¶

    Sentrik integrates with every major CI platform via a single shell command:

    # GitHub Actions
- name: Sentrik Compliance Gate
  run: sentrik gate --git-range origin/main...HEAD --decorate-pr --status-check

    Native integrations are available for GitHub Actions (published to the GitHub Actions Marketplace as maxgerhardson/sentrik-community@v1), Azure Pipelines, and GitLab CI. The gate posts inline PR comments, creates commit status checks, and exits non-zero on failure — blocking merge without requiring any custom integration code.

    VS Code Extension¶

    The Sentrik VS Code extension scans on every file save and surfaces findings as native VS Code diagnostics — the same squiggly underlines used for TypeScript errors. Engineers see compliance violations as they write code, before a PR is opened. The extension connects automatically to GitHub Copilot (no API key required) for AI-powered fix suggestions on any finding.

    MCP Server for AI Agents¶

    Sentrik exposes an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server that AI coding agents can call during code generation. When Claude Code, Cursor, or Cline is writing code for a regulated project, it can query Sentrik for the applicable compliance rules before writing a line — so generated code passes the gate on the first try rather than requiring a remediation cycle.

    The MCP server is available in the official MCP registry (io.github.maxgerhardson/sentrik) and is compatible with any MCP-enabled agent or tool.

    Work Item Integration¶

    When Sentrik finds requirement drift — code that diverges from documented requirements — it creates work items automatically in GitHub Issues, Azure DevOps, or Jira: assigned, labeled, linked to the specific finding, and ready for triage. No manual translation from scan output to ticket.

    Governance Profiles¶

    Sentrik ships with three governance profiles that control the balance between autonomy and oversight:

    Strict — Human review required for critical findings and requirement changes. Auto-patching capped at low severity. Designed for Class C medical devices, aviation software, and defense-regulated environments.

    Standard — Human review on critical findings. Auto-patching permitted up to medium severity. Appropriate for most regulated SaaS and healthcare applications.

    Permissive — Maximum agent autonomy. Human review gates disabled. Suitable for development and pre-production environments.

    Profiles are declared in .sentrik/config.yaml and can be overridden per environment via GUARD_GOVERNANCE_PROFILE.

    Audit Evidence Generation¶

    Compliance Reports¶

    sentrik compliance-report -f "IEC 62304"

    Per-framework compliance reports show coverage, finding counts by severity, gate history, and trend over time. Reports are generated in HTML for sharing with auditors and JSON for GRC integration.

    Trust Center¶

    sentrik trust-center --org "Acme Medical"

    A public-safe compliance page summarizing your organization's compliance posture, suitable for embedding in a customer trust portal or sharing with procurement reviewers.

    Audit-Ready Export¶

    sentrik evidence-export -f SOC2

    Produces a ZIP bundle containing the Evidence Map, compliance report, attestation chain, and scan history — packaged for submission to auditors without requiring access to the codebase or CI system.

    Audit Log¶

    Every governance action — scan, gate decision, attestation, suppression, approval — is recorded in an append-only audit log with HMAC-SHA256 integrity signatures on each entry. The log is machine-readable (JSONL) and designed to be acceptable as evidence in regulated audit proceedings.

    Code Intelligence¶

    Beyond compliance enforcement, Sentrik builds a structural understanding of your codebase that improves over time.

    Quality Scoring — A 0–100 quality score across six dimensions: compliance, complexity, test coverage, documentation, consistency, and dependency health. Quality gates can be set in CI: sentrik quality-score --min-quality 70.

    Project Profiling — Sentrik auto-detects languages, frameworks, architecture patterns, and coding conventions. The project profile is used to scope rule applicability and to inform the MCP context provided to AI agents.

    STRIDE Threat Modeling — LLM-powered analysis of code for Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, and Elevation of Privilege threats. Findings are tracked, discussed with AI, and mitigated within the dashboard.

    Design Decision Auditing — Sentrik identifies and records significant design decisions from code changes, surfaces them for review, and maintains an acknowledged decision log — satisfying IEC 62304 and DO-178C requirements for architecture documentation.

    Expertise Tracking — Developer expertise profiles built from git history. When a PR touches code outside the author's domain of expertise, Sentrik flags it for senior review.

    Deployment¶

    Sentrik is distributed as a Python package and runs entirely on-premises. There is no data exfiltration, no cloud scanning service, and no dependency on Sentrik infrastructure for day-to-day operation.

    pip install sentrik
sentrik init
sentrik scan

    Air-gapped environments are supported via offline license validation. The license is HMAC-signed and validated locally — no network call required after initial activation.

    License tiers are enforced locally. The free tier (6 packs, 193 rules) is permanent and requires no license key. Team and Organization tiers are activated with an HMAC-signed license key validated locally; when a network is available, paid keys are additionally verified against the licensing portal to catch revoked keys, and validation degrades gracefully offline so air-gapped environments keep working without interruption.

    Docker support is available for containerized deployment of the REST API and dashboard.

    The Sentrik Dashboard¶

    The Sentrik web dashboard (sentrik dashboard) provides a compliance management interface accessible from any browser, including from within VS Code as a native webview panel. The dashboard includes 21 pages across six sections:

    • Monitoring — Overview, Findings, Vulnerabilities, History
    • Compliance — Rules browser, Standards Packs, Reports, License compliance, Evidence Map
    • Integrations — Work item tracking, DevOps provider connections (Azure DevOps, GitHub, Jira)
    • Governance — Policies, Approvals, Audit Log, Attestation
    • Intelligence — Quality Score, Project Profile, Design Decisions, Expertise, Threat Model
    • Settings — Full configuration viewer

    All dashboard data is generated from local scan output and attestation history — no data leaves the machine.

    Who Sentrik Is Built For¶

    Medical device software teams operating under IEC 62304, ISO 14971, IEC 81001-5-1, and 21 CFR Part 11 face the most demanding compliance requirements in software engineering. Sentrik was designed from the ground up to enforce these frameworks at the code level, generate the evidence they require, and do so without disrupting development velocity.

    Fintech and payments teams under SOC 2, PCI DSS, and ISO 27001 need continuous compliance posture without the six-week evidence sprint that precedes every audit. Sentrik's attestation chain and Evidence Map replace that sprint with an always-current, auditor-ready record.

    AI product teams building under the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF need to enforce transparency, data governance, human oversight, and prohibited practice requirements in code — requirements that have no tooling equivalent in the current market. Sentrik's EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF packs are the only code-level enforcement available for these frameworks.

    Defense contractors meeting CMMC 2.0 and NIST 800-53 requirements need enforcement that runs in air-gapped and classified environments. Sentrik's offline license validation and on-premises architecture make this possible without any cloud dependency.

    Sentrik is available immediately on the free tier with no license key required.

    pip install sentrik
sentrik init
sentrik scan

    The free tier includes OWASP Top 10, SOC 2, Python Security, Go Security, Supply Chain Security, and C/C++ Coding Standards — 193 rules, permanently free.

    Team and Organization tiers covering the full 24-framework, 595-rule library are available by contacting the Sentrik team. Air-gapped deployments, custom rule pack development, and compliance audit support are available under the Organization and Enterprise tiers.
